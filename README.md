One 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of **Monday 23 November 2026**, drawn
with the day's own figures: Zelenskyy's claim that seven Ukrainian sea drones sank
the Russian corvette *Merkury* at Benghazi, the Kyiv region day of mourning as the
Dymer death toll reaches eleven and a 79-year-old woman is pulled out alive, the Air
Force's overnight tally of 153 drones with 134 downed or suppressed, the eight MoUs
and 24,000-crore-rupee package Narendra Modi and Balendra Shah announced in Delhi
alongside the inaugural Saryu–Kamala Express, the North Atlantic Council that met in
Brussels on Polish airspace, and the rebuilt Shuri Castle main hall that opened to
the public at noon in Naha. Two equal lead panels carry the leads, four tiles carry
the rest, and the ink band at the foot marks the big weekend stories that were *not*
Monday's news. Every mark is original — pictograms built from primitives, one colour
per story, type sized to stay readable at 300 pixels across. `tools/render.py`
(Pillow) draws the picture locally; `main.py` is standard library only and copies the
finished `entry.png` to the output directory the grader gives it.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Redraw the image (needs Pillow, local only): `python3 tools/render.py`
