One 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of **Monday 23 November 2026**: the
Kyiv region day of mourning as the Dymer toll rises to seven, drones over Ukraine
through the night, the North Atlantic Council meeting in Brussels, Nepal's prime
minister meeting Narendra Modi in Delhi, the rebuilt Shuri Castle main hall opening
at noon in Okinawa, and the DNA identification work after the Indonesian factory
fire. Every tile is drawn from scratch — original pictograms, one colour per story,
sized to stay readable at 300 pixels across — and a yellow band at the foot marks
the big weekend stories that were *not* Monday's news. `tools/render.py` (Pillow)
draws the picture locally; `main.py` is standard library only and copies the
finished `entry.png` to the output directory the grader gives it.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Redraw the image (needs Pillow, local only): `python3 tools/render.py`
